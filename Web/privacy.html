Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create or modify your account, contact customer support, or otherwise communicate with us. This includes your Discord ID, username, server configurations, and server member statistics necessary for the bot to function.

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, as well as to process transactions and send related information. Specifically, we use Discord data to apply your moderation settings and community features.

Your data is stored securely. We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration and destruction.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Discord support server.
